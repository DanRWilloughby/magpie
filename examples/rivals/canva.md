# Rival dossier — Canva

> **This one is real.** Every quote below comes from a captured page under `raw/canva/<date>/` — the 2026-08-19 snapshot from the live site, the 2025-11-06 baseline recovered from the Internet Archive (cited). It also demonstrates a second kind of rival: Canva doesn't make a time-tracking tool, but its **free invoice generator** is what this library's example owner actually loses deals to. Your most dangerous competitor is sometimes the giant free tool your customers already have open — worth a dossier even when it's not a lookalike.

## Snapshot: 2025-11-06 (baseline — Wayback Machine, pricing page only)

**Pricing + packaging.** Free · Pro "€110 /year for one person" (marked "Intro price") · Business "€160/year per person" · Enterprise contact-sales. (Archive crawl rendered EUR — don't compare prices across snapshots' currencies.) [raw/canva/2025-11-06]

**AI packaging.** Sold as vague tier words: Free = "Limited AI access", Pro = "High AI access", Business/Enterprise = "Higher AI access"; "AI usage is pooled across tools and resets monthly." No numeric caps, no paid AI add-on anywhere on the page. [raw/canva/2025-11-06]

**Third-party rails.** Business/Enterprise advertise "Access to Leonardo.Ai Apprentice plan (Soon)" and "Access to Flourish Presenter plan (Soon)". Free tier: "1 Brand Kit for colors, logos, and fonts". [raw/canva/2025-11-06]

## Snapshot: 2026-08-19 (live capture)

**Positioning (their words).** Homepage H1: "What will you design today?" — "Make AI-powered social posts, videos, presentations, and more with Canva." Frame is the everything-suite: "All the tools. All in one place. Meet the Visual Suite… powered by AI." Title tag: "Canva: Visual Suite for Everyone". [raw/canva/2026-08-19]

**The surface that overlaps ours.** "Free Online Invoice Generator" — "create professional invoices in minutes", "add secure payment links for your customers", template categories include Freelance and Contractor, "+75M customers trust Canva Worldwide". [raw/canva/2026-08-19]

**Features / capabilities — vs ours.**

| Capability | Canva | Ours |
|---|---|---|
| Invoice creation | Free templates + generator, any design | Generated from tracked hours |
| Payment links on invoices | Yes — free tier | Yes |
| Invoices tied to time tracking | No — documents, not workflow | Yes — the core |
| Reminders / overdue tracking | No (folders, manual) | Yes |
| Brand polish on the invoice | World-class, their whole game | Basic templates |

**Pricing + packaging.** Free "US$0" · Pro "US$180 /year for one person" · Business "US$250 /year per person" (Recommended) · Enterprise contact-sales. AI is now a metered allowance: Free "Up to 200 Standard AI uses or 20 Premium AI uses", Pro "10x more AI than Canva Free", Business "20x" — plus a paid **AI Pass** add-on ("40x more AI than Canva Pro – or 20x more than Canva Business") and "AI top-ups (extra cost)". [raw/canva/2026-08-19]

**Who they sell to.** Everyone ("Visual Suite for Everyone"), with a small-business lane called out: "Built for small businesses with big aspirations." The pricing FAQ still answers "What happened to Canva Teams?" — packaging consolidation is recent enough to need explaining. [raw/canva/2026-08-19]

**Strengths to respect.** The invoice generator is genuinely good enough for a freelancer's first year — free, branded, payment links included. And the suite gravity is real: once a freelancer's proposals and social posts live there, the invoice defaults there too.

**Weaknesses to exploit.** An invoice in Canva is a *document*, not a *workflow* — nothing connects hours worked to money billed, nothing chases late payers, "keep track" means folders. The moment a freelancer bills more than a couple of clients, designing each invoice by hand is the manual work our product exists to delete.

**Counter-or-adopt.**
- **Counter:** position against the document-vs-workflow gap — "your invoice shouldn't be a design project every month."
- **Adopt:** payment links on the free tier set the floor — ours can never be a paid feature.
- **Watch:** if invoices ever join their AI-generation surface ("draft my invoice from this email thread"), the wedge narrows.

### What changed since 2025-11-06

- **AI went from adjective to meter.** Tier words ("Limited/High/Higher AI access") → numeric allowances ("Up to 200 Standard AI uses", "10x/20x more than Free") plus a paid AI Pass add-on and top-ups. Ten months ago AI was a plan feature; now it's a second bill.
- **Free tier quietly tightened.** "1 Brand Kit for colors, logos, and fonts" → "1 Brand Kit (3 colors only)".
- **Third-party rails swapped for in-house.** "Access to Leonardo.Ai Apprentice plan (Soon)" is gone; the new-features slot now sells "Canva AI 2.0", "Canva AI connectors", and "Components". Flourish graduated from "(Soon)" to shipped on Business.
- **Pro's "Intro price" tag is gone.**

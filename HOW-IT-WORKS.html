Magpie 🪶 — how it works

Feed it anything you're consuming. It asks one question — what's this to you? — and files the result in a plain-markdown library you own. No cloud, no accounts.

/magpie <anything>

A YouTube link, an article, a competitor's site, a pasted thread, a local file — or append URLs to queue.md all week and say "process my queue".

Your library — ~/Documents/Magpie, plain markdown, Obsidian-friendly

notes/ one note per source · rivals/ one dossier per competitor · raw/ original transcripts & snapshots · digests/ weekly rollups · profile.md your context · voice.md your voice

Your profile (from the one-question onboarding) is what makes it smart: it knows who you are, what you build, and who you compete with — so it can suggest the right lens and map "them vs you".

Tool overview — everything you can say

SayWhat happens
/magpie startOne-question onboarding. Drop a few links that represent you (or just talk) — it drafts your profile, you confirm. ~2 minutes.
/magpie <url>Captures it, asks what it is to you, applies the lens, files it. Add the lens inline to skip the question: /magpie <url> steal
"tear down this competitor"Rival lens — builds or updates that competitor's living dossier and shows what changed since last time.
… content --respondContent teardown plus a drafted reply/post in your voice (fill in voice.md once; never auto-posts).
/magpie digestRollup of everything since last digest: new captures, top signals, rival changes, one thing worth revisiting.
/magpie forget <name>Cleanly un-know something — quarantined for 14 days, never instantly deleted.
/magpie doctorHealth check: what's working, what's not, exactly how to fix it.

Local-first by design: no accounts, no cloud, no required API keys. Your knowledge stays in your files — readable by any tool, owned by you. 🪶
